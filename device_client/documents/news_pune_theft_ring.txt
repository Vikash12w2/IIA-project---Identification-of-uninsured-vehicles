Pune police close in on car theft ring targeting sedans

Pune, 15 September 2026. Police in Pune say a gang operating around Shivaji Nagar and Katraj has stolen at least six sedans since July, mostly Skoda and Hyundai models. The cars are fitted with cloned number plates and moved out of the city within days. A black Skoda Rapid stolen in August was spotted by a traffic camera at Shivaji Nagar in September. Inspector R. Jadhav said the ANPR network has helped track the vehicles.
