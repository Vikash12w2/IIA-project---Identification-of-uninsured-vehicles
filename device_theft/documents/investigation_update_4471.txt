Investigation update on FIR-2026-4471 (stolen black Skoda Rapid)

9 September 2026. The ANPR control room informed the station that camera CAM-016 at Shivaji Nagar read a plate MH14ZZ0O00 on a black Skoda sedan at 10:10 hrs. The read had low confidence and the last character group may have been misread. The location is less than a kilometre from where the car was stolen.

A patrol reached the junction within twenty minutes but the vehicle had left. Officers suspect the gang reported in the city news, which changes number plates on stolen sedans before moving them out of Pune. Insurance company ICICI Lombard has asked for a copy of the FIR for the claim filed by the owner.
