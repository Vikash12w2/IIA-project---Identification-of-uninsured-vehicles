Industry fraud bulletin, September 2026

Insurers in Pune have seen a rise in theft claims for Skoda and Hyundai sedans where the stolen car is later seen on the road with a changed or cloned number plate. Claims teams should check ANPR sightings after the theft date before settling a claim, and should match the chassis number in the RC with the vehicle inspected. Shivaji Nagar and Katraj areas account for most of the reported cases.
